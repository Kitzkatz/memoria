# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Memoria is an open-source project intended to encourage experimentation, criticism, and technical discussion around memory systems, information retrieval, ranking, and related infrastructure.

Contributors and participants are expected to interact respectfully and in good faith.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

* Be respectful and constructive when discussing ideas, implementations, and results.
* Critique code, architecture, methodology, benchmarks, and technical claims rather than people.
* Clearly distinguish facts, measurements, assumptions, and opinions.
* Be open to changing conclusions when new evidence is presented.
* Provide actionable feedback when possible.
* Respect different levels of experience and background.
* Assume good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
* Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, personal attacks, threats, or intimidation.
* Discriminatory or hateful language directed at individuals or groups.
* Deliberate disruption of project discussions.
* Publishing private or identifying information about another person without permission.
* Trolling or deliberately provoking participants.
* Misrepresenting another participant's statements or work.
* Criticizing or dismissing contributors based solely on credentials, employment status, or lack of formal academic affiliation.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation hostile or unsafe.

## Technical Criticism

Memoria is explicitly intended to receive technical criticism.

You are encouraged to question:

* Architectural decisions.
* Retrieval and ranking strategies.
* Scheduler and completion-policy design.
* Benchmark methodology.
* Performance claims.
* Experimental assumptions.
* Abstractions and implementation complexity.
* Comparisons with existing systems.
* Documentation and reproducibility.

Strong disagreement is acceptable when it remains focused on the technical subject.

Statements such as "this abstraction is unnecessary" or "this benchmark does not demonstrate what you think it demonstrates" are useful contributions when supported by reasoning or evidence.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers will make a reasonable effort to:

* Keep discussions focused on the project.
* Consider criticism in good faith.
* Explain technical decisions when practical.
* Remove or address abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior.
* Resolve moderation issues at their discretion when necessary to keep the project usable.

Maintainers are not required to agree with feedback in order to consider it valuable.

## Reporting

If you encounter harassment, abuse, or other conduct that violates this Code of Conduct, please open an issue only if the matter is appropriate for public discussion. Otherwise, contact the project maintainer privately through the contact method provided in the repository.

Please do not use public issues to disclose sensitive personal information or security vulnerabilities.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issue discussions, pull requests, and other project spaces where participants are representing or directly contributing to Memoria.

Thank you for helping make technical disagreement useful rather than personal.
